## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-26

### Added

- First public release: a ChromaDB v2 client with Ollama embeddings, a scoped semantic cache and
  HyDE, published from the `nuvek-core` repository under the MIT licence. Endpoints, models and
  the tenant path come from the caller's `ChromaConfig`.
